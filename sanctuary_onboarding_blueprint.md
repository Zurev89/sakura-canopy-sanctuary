# The Sakura Canopy Sanctuary: Architectural Layout & Onboarding Blueprint

## Section I: The Spatial Infrastructure (The Faction Hub Architecture)
To ensure the absolute sovereignty and creative freedom of every creator, the physical layout of the platform rejects linear lists and corporate dashboard designs. It is modeled as a massive, circular radial station where distinct creative ideas can thrive side by side without choking each other out.

### 1. The Central Neutral Concourse
* **The Shared Commons:** A grounded, central circular room serving as a shared meeting place for all visitors. It features warm stone floorboards, deep-seated common couches, and soft, comforting candlelight.
* **Algorithmic Neutrality:** No single aesthetic, narrative tree, or philosophy dominates the center. It serves purely as an atmospheric, non-commercial town square.
* **The Perimeter Corridors:** Branching outward from this central ring are distinct, sectioned-off radial spokes or docking bays.

### 2. The Autonomous Radial Spokes (Individual Booths)
* **Complete Creative Sovereignty:** Each creator is granted a self-contained sector or pod. The interior wall textures, lighting, and environmental atmosphere transform completely to match the core identity of that specific booth's proposal.
* **Zero Cross-Interference:** Individual spokes are separated by thick architectural arches or transitional thresholds. A gritty, monochrome tactical sector never bleeds into a vibrant fantasy pod.
* **The Primary Seed Module (The Hufflepuff Haven):** The foundational booth next to the main foyer entrance is styled directly after an earthy sanctuary. It features low circular windows, exposed brick, hanging ivy, and a warm hearth. Overhead, the massive branches of the Iron Sakura tree spread its canopy across this dedicated sector, allowing soft pink petals to drift within its boundary and float out gently into the shared central perimeter.

## Section II: The Welcome Inscription & Orientation Portal
When an individual leaves the outside corporate grind and enters the domain, they are met with zero commercial data-harvesting or aggressive monetization barriers. They land in a stone-vaulted entryway where the absolute rules of the sanctuary are explicitly declared.

### 1. The Orientation Hearth Inscription
Etched permanently into a grand central mantelpiece is the foundational declaration of intent:

"Welcome and thank you for coming to this place. This is an open sanctuary for stories, ideas, and the people who dream them. We have built a circular station where creative minds can grow separate, sovereign worlds without corporate interference. Here, there are no artificial timelines, no frantic metrics, and not a single dime will ever be expected of you. You are not a product. You are a gardener. Take a breath, look around, and share what you carry."

### 2. The Avatar Loom (Cost-Free Identity Creation)
To establish deep, narrative citizenship without forcing users to link real-world corporate data profiles, visitors are given complete access to a magical identity creation suite:
* **The No-Monetization Pledge:** 100% of the asset items are fully unlocked from day one. There are zero premium cosmetic items, microtransactions, or hidden subscription gates. Not a single cent will ever be requested.
* **Organic Sketch Esthetics:** Users stitch together a personal profile avatar utilizing stylized, hand-drawn natural assets—such as heavy traveler cloaks pinned with individual Sakura blossoms, ink-stained cartographer gloves, or leather-bound writer satchels.
* **Ecosystem Archetype Sorting:** Upon completion of their visual identity, citizens complete a brief philosophical questionnaire to discover their place within the living orchard:
  * **The Planter:** Dedicated to generating raw blueprint proposals, narrative fixes, and structural alternatives.
  * **The Tender:** Dedicated to analyzing peer scripts, debugging plot armor issues, and supporting fellow creators.
  * **The Wanderer:** Dedicated to gathering copies of the ideas from the booths and carrying them with the digital winds to the outside world.
