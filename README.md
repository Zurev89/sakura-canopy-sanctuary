# sakura-canopy-sanctuary

A blueprint for an open-source, non-profit digital sanctuary for storytelling — designed to protect creators, side characters, and narrative integrity from corporate exploitation.

> “Welcome and thank you for coming to this place. We are a forest, not a factory.”

This is not a product. It is a living room that expands when more people arrive.  
No money is ever required. No data is harvested. No algorithm decides what is visible.

### The three core documents

1. **[The Genesis Log](the_journey_and_genesis.md)**  
   The complete thought process: Naruto’s loss of relatability, the 12–18 month Shippuden compression, Itachi’s ignored warning, Kakashi as the model of earned adult weight, Boruto as Infinite Tsukuyomi, and the decision to plant a new tree outside the existing system.

2. **[The Core Manifesto](sakura_canopy_blueprint.md)**  
   The gardener philosophy, the rejection of forced power-scaling, and the structural principles of the sanctuary.

3. **[Onboarding & Architecture](sanctuary_onboarding_blueprint.md)**  
   How the circular station actually works: neutral commons, radial sovereign booths, free avatar system, and the absolute zero-cost pledge.
