# 🌸 The Sakura Canopy Sanctuary 🌸

> **"Welcome and thank you for coming to this place. We are a forest, not a factory."**

This is an open-source, non-profit digital sanctuary for storytelling, designed to protect creators, side characters, and narrative integrity from corporate exploitation. We believe that true sequential art is a living ecosystem—not a sterile corporate assembly line.

---

## 🏛️ The Architecture of the Station
This repository serves as a conceptual blueprint for a decentralized, crowdsourced creative platform. Modeled after a circular space station or a radial guild hall, this space is designed to let distinct ideas grow side by side without choking each other out:

*   **The Neutral Commons:** A shared, quiet entryway where creators and fans gather as equals.
*   **The Elastic Living Room:** A modular digital hub that smoothly expands to add new "booths" as more visionaries join the movement.
*   **The Radial Spokes:** Individual, compartmentalized alcoves where creators have complete sovereignty over their aesthetic—whether it's a cozy, plant-filled Hufflepuff hearth under the Iron Sakura or a dark, high-stakes tactical grid.
*   **100% Cost-Free Citizenship:** Inspired by the onboarding charm of Pottermore, users can craft fluid, natural sketch-style avatars without premium tiers, data tracking, or microtransactions. **Not a single dime will ever be expected of anyone who comes here.**
