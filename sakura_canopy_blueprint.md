# The Sakura Canopy Blueprint  
## Core principles of the sanctuary

### 1. Everything must grow
Stories, characters, and creators are living systems. Growth requires time, seasons of rest, real consequences, and shared sunlight. Forced acceleration produces sterile results.

### 2. Earned weight over spectacle
A character’s seriousness should send a shiver because the audience knows the cost was paid in years of lived experience, limitation, and loss — not because a new glowing form was unlocked.

### 3. Collective over isolated godhood
Victory that depends on one or two demigods invalidates the very lessons the story claims to teach. Side characters and supporting systems must be allowed to mature into genuine contributors.

### 4. Demographics as seasons, not prisons
A story may begin in a youthful register and mature into adult complexity as its characters and its creator age. Rigid magazine categories should not freeze that process.

### 5. Outside the machine
The sanctuary is deliberately planted outside existing corporate publishing structures. It does not seek to reform the old system from within; it offers a parallel ecosystem that creators and readers can choose when they are ready.

### 6. Zero extraction
No one is charged. No one is tracked for advertising. No one is required to create an account that feeds a commercial database. The space exists to shelter and to share, nothing more.
